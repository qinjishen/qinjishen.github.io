Welcome to the blog.

This site will collect notes on reading, study, technical practice, and long-term projects. The publishing workflow stays intentionally simple: posts begin in Obsidian, remain in Markdown, and are built into public pages by GitHub.

## What comes next

- knowledge organization and visualization
- reading and research notes
- progress reports from personal projects
- practical experience with technical tools

This post also serves as a template. Duplicate its frontmatter, then change the title, date, and permalink to publish the next piece.
